Smart Ambulance Routing

AI-Based Smart Emergency Ambulance Routing and Treatment-Ready Hospital Selection System.

Presented By:

Monika.R.K • Sahana.S • Rubini.P • Nigidha.B

Academic Accreditation:

Approved by AICTE | Affiliated to Anna University | ISO 9001:2008

01

Abstract

Quick ambulance response is essential, but the nearest hospital may lack required facilities. Our AI selects the best hospital based on patient condition, beds, specialists, equipment, blood, and traffic to minimize treatment delays and hospital transfers.

02

Problem Statement

Hospitals frequently lack ICU/emergency beds, required specialists, equipment (e.g. CT scan), or blood matching the patient's group. Relying on distance alone results in treatment delays and dangerous inter-hospital transfers.

03

Technology Stack

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Tailwind
  • AI/ML: Python, Scikit-learn, Ranking Model
  • Maps & Routing: OpenStreetMap, Leaflet.js
  • Deployment: Cloud Native / Render.com
04

Expected Results

Substantially reduced treatment delays, 100% facility readiness upon arrival, traffic-aware real-time dynamic re-routing, eliminated secondary transfers, and seamless ambulance-to-hospital coordination.

05

Future Scope

Integration with government emergency services, green-light traffic signal preemption (Green Corridor), predictive bed availability via time-series forecasting, and voice assistance for paramedics.

06

Core Novelty

“Patient-specific Treatment Readiness Score-based Hospital Selection with Real-Time Traffic, Medical Resource, Bed, and Blood Availability.”
